An exploratory data analysis (EDA) of Walmart’s historical weekly sales data to identify seasonal revenue patterns, holiday impacts, and store-level performance disparities.

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

* **Holiday Surge:** Discovered an **8% increase** in average weekly sales during holiday weeks, with peak revenue occurring in **December**.
* **Seasonal Lows:** Identified consistent dips in sales volume during the **May–June** period.
* **Store Performance Gap:** Uncovered a **518.3% variance** in total sales between the top 5 and bottom 5 performing stores, highlighting key opportunities for targeted operational and inventory strategies.
